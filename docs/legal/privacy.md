# Nimble Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [fill in before publishing]_

Nimble ("we", "us") is a coaching app that connects coaches and their clients.
This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

## What we collect

- **Account info**: name, email, role (coach or client), and the workspace you belong to.
- **Coaching content**: workouts, programs, assignments, exercise library entries, comments,
  and the results you log (sets, reps, weight, time, distance) while training.
- **Device info, if you enable notifications**: a push token tied to your device, so we can
  deliver workout reminders. We do not use it for anything else.
- **Usage data**: basic crash and performance diagnostics, so we can fix bugs.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or browsing history, and we do not sell your
data to anyone.

## Why we collect it

- To run the app: show your workouts, track your progress, and let your coach assign programs.
- To notify you: a push notification when your coach assigns something new.
- To keep the service working: diagnosing crashes and errors.

## Who can see it

- Your coach can see the workouts and results you log, so they can coach you.
- Clients in your workspace cannot see each other's data.
- We (the people operating Nimble) can access data only to provide support or fix problems.
- We use [Supabase](https://supabase.com) to host the database and [Resend](https://resend.com)
  to deliver transactional email (sign-up confirmation, password reset). Their own privacy
  policies apply to data in transit through those services.
- If your coach or workspace uses the in-app assistant, the messages you send it may be
  processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. We do not use your data to
  train any model.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your profile,
your logged results, and (if you are a coach) your workspace and everyone in it.

## Your choices

- **Notifications**: turn push notifications on or off at any time in Settings.
- **Delete your account**: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This is permanent and cannot
  be undone. If you are a coach, deleting your account also deletes every client in your
  workspace, since the workspace cannot exist without its coach.
- **Export or correct your data**: contact us at the address below.

## Children

Nimble is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
